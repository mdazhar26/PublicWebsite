ArrowsBlock Blast!+ Support

Get help with Arrow Quest, Block Boom, Word Puzzle, Sudoku, Color Sort, Block Shift, and Tic Tac Toe.

Contact us

Email support@get-set-go.in. Include the game name, app version, device model, and what happened. Screenshots are helpful. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Remove Ads subscription

Open Settings from the game selection page, then choose Remove Ads. The yearly subscription removes banner and between-level advertisements in all seven games while active. The purchase dialog displays your App Store price.

Restore purchases

Use the Apple Account used for the purchase. Open Settings → Remove Ads or Ads Removed → Restore purchases. Subscriptions must be active to restore ad-free access.

Manage or cancel your subscription

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Select this subscription to manage or cancel renewal. Apple subscription help.

Game help and saved progress

Use the question-mark button inside a game for its instructions. Game progress is stored on your device; deleting the app may remove local progress. Manage device backups separately.
