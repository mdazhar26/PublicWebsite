SEEKERS QUEST / YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This policy explains how Seekers Quest handles information when you visit get-set-go.in or use Arrow Quest: Way Out. The game information below describes version 1.2.0.

1. Information stored in the game

Arrow Quest does not require an account. It saves game progress and preferences on your device, including unlocked levels, scores, stars, coins, lives, play streaks, the last played date, and sound, music, vibration, and theme settings. It also stores level data and tutorial preferences to support gameplay.

This information is used to resume your progress and remember your choices. The current game does not include a Seekers Quest account service or a feature that uploads this progress to our servers. Your device’s backup services may handle app data according to your device settings.

2. Optional feedback and support

If you email us, we receive your email address, any name supplied by your email service, and the content you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate problems.

The game’s feedback feature prepares an email containing your rating, remarks, app version, device model, operating system version, and country or locale derived from your device’s language and region settings. The field labelled “Mobile Location” refers to that region or locale, not GPS coordinates.

The feedback details are passed to Outlook or its web compose service to prepare your message. You can review, edit, or cancel the message before sending it to us. The email provider processes compose and message information under its own privacy terms. Please avoid including passwords or other sensitive information in feedback.

3. Website visits and cookies

This website does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts, account registration, or contact forms. Its own code does not set cookies or use browser storage to track visitors. The artwork and trailer are served directly with the website.

When the website is served through GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address and browser information, to deliver and protect its services. Hosting activity is subject to GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

4. Third-party services

The current game source does not include an advertising or analytics SDK. App stores, operating system services, and email providers may separately process information when you download or update the game, back up your device, or contact us. Their own privacy policies apply to those services.

Opening an external link takes you to a service with its own data practices. This policy describes Seekers Quest’s website and game, not the independent practices of those providers.

5. Storage, retention, and your choices

You can remove locally saved game information by clearing the app’s storage where your device supports it, or by deleting the app and its data. This also removes saved progress. Device or cloud backups may need to be managed separately.

Support correspondence is stored by the email services used to send and receive it. For questions about retained correspondence, or to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent us, email mdazhar26@gmail.com. We will review requests in accordance with applicable requirements; we may need to verify that a request concerns your information.

You can use the game without sending feedback. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply. Contact us if you would like to exercise a right or raise a privacy concern.

6. Children’s privacy

The game does not ask players to create a profile or provide their name, age, or contact details to play. Parents and guardians should supervise any optional email feedback from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can review it and address removal where appropriate.

7. Security

Local game information is stored within your device’s app storage. Protect your device and email account with appropriate access controls. No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or game changes. The latest version will be available on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Seekers Quest

For privacy questions or requests, email mdazhar26@gmail.com. Please mention “Privacy” and the relevant website or game in your message.
